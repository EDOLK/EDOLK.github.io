# Rogue Connector Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Rogue Connector has no accounts and doesn't collect personal information itself. It shows banner ads through Google AdMob, and it sells a one-time "remove ads" purchase through Google Play.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

- The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), app interactions such as ad views and taps, device and network information (such as device model, Android version and connection type), and diagnostic information.
- Google uses this data as described in its own policy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it's required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice any time under settings → privacy options.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.
- Once Google Play confirms your "remove ads" purchase, the app stops loading ads and stops sending data to AdMob. After a reinstall or on a new device, ads may show briefly until the app has restored your purchase from Google Play.

## Purchases (Google Play)

- Google Play handles the "remove ads" purchase and payment. Rogue Connector never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase, so it can restore it on a reinstall or a new device. If a purchase is refunded, ads come back.
- Google Play's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## Data on your device

- Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
- If your device's own Android backup is turned on, Android may include this saved data in that backup. Google handles that backup, not Rogue Connector.

## Children

Rogue Connector isn't directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
